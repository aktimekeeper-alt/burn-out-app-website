Everything you need to know about Burnout

Burnout is a social networking app built specifically for car enthusiasts. It's designed to help you showcase your build, find local car meets, and connect with other gearheads who share your passion. Think of it as the ultimate hub for car culture.

We're targeting Winter 2025 for our initial launch. Join the waitlist to get early access and be the first to know when we go live!

Yes! Burnout will be free to download and use. We may offer optional premium features in the future, but the core experience of connecting with other enthusiasts will always be free.

Digital Car Cards are your vehicle's profile on Burnout. They showcase your ride with photos, detailed specs, mod lists, and build history. It's like a trading card for your car that you can share with others at meets.

When you meet someone at a car show or meet, you can tap phones to instantly exchange Car Cards. It's like trading physical cards, but digital. Build your collection of cards from all the amazing builds you encounter!

All of them! Whether you're into JDM, Euro, American Muscle, classics, trucks, exotics, stance, off-road, or anything with wheels - Burnout is for you. We celebrate all aspects of car culture.

Our Live Meet Finder uses real-time location sharing to show you car meets, cruises, and shows happening near you right now. You can also browse upcoming events and follow your local car scenes.

Proximity Chat lets you connect with other Burnout users who are physically nearby at events. When you're at a car meet, you can chat with everyone around you - no need to exchange numbers or social handles.

Burnout includes built-in event planning tools. You can create meets, set waypoints for cruises, invite your crew, and manage RSVPs all within the app. Rally your local scene with just a few taps.
